The game

Build it, leave it, come back to more.

  1. Light the altar

    Your island starts with a single building: the Spark Altar, the heart of your civilization. Everything else grows around it.

  2. Build and let it run

    Houses bring villagers. Woodcutters, quarries and fields keep producing while you are away; come back, collect, and upgrade.

  3. Raise the core

    The level of your core sets your era. Reach the next one and every building on the island transforms.

Season one

Four eras, one island.

  1. ERA I

    Village

    Core: Spark Altar

    Timber, stone and the first houses around the altar.

  2. ERA II

    Town

    Core: Town Keep · from core level 4

    The altar becomes a keep. Stone mansions, windmills and a busier farm.

  3. ERA III

    Metropolis

    Core: Metro Tower · from core level 7

    Paved streets, towers and street lights that come on at dusk.

  4. ERA IV

    Star Age

    Core: Star Base · from core level 10

    Domes, landing pads and a cargo shuttle. The island looks up.

Each new era changes how every building looks, and the island grows with it. The pictures are in-game screenshots from a development build. What comes after the Star Age belongs to later seasons and is not announced yet.

Island life

More than numbers going up.

  • Villagers and a day that turns

    Every house adds a villager. They work, sit on the benches you place, and the lamps come on when night falls.

  • Quests and relics

    Daily, weekly and monthly quests to finish, and relics to find and equip.

  • A farm island

    A second island for your animals, joined by a bridge. Upgrade it to make room for new ones.

  • Pets, benches and lights

    Pets to adopt, and decorations you can place, turn and move.

  • A look for every era

    Costumes change how your whole island looks, from the first village to the Star Age.

  • Ranks and a leaderboard

    Rank comes from developing your island. Climb the leaderboard and plant a language flag on your shore.

  • Islands to visit

    Tour the islands of players on the leaderboard, and wake the Echo Isles drifting around your own.

  • A mailbox and gift codes

    A mailbox for letters and gifts, and codes that unlock in-game presents.

GTP

Genesis Testnet Point.

  • It is an in-game point. GTP has no monetary value. It is not a token and it is not for sale.
  • It comes only from rewarded ads you choose to watch, in the Telegram version of the game.
  • It does not buy a lead. Rank comes from developing your island; badges, costumes, pets and decorations change how things look, not how fast you produce.

Nothing on this site is financial advice.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

When can I play?

There is no date yet. The game is in development for the web and for Telegram.

Do I have to keep it open?

No. Your island keeps producing while you are away, until its storage is full. Come back, collect, and build on.

Is GTP a cryptocurrency?

No. GTP stands for Genesis Testnet Point. It is an in-game point with no monetary value.

Where does it run?

In a web browser and inside Telegram, on computers and phones.

Which languages?

The game is translated into 50 languages. This site is in English for now.
