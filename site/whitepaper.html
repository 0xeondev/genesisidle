01Summary

Genesis Idle is an idle civilization builder. You begin with one island and a Spark Altar, and you grow that island through four eras, from a village to a star base.

The game opens first inside Telegram; a web version follows. It is free. It sells nothing to players, and nothing in it can be bought to get ahead.

During the testnet, players collect Genesis Testnet Points (GTP), a record of how much they have taken part. This paper explains how the game works, what GTP is and is not, how the project pays for itself, how play is kept fair, and what comes next.

This is a living document. It describes the game as it is being built and will be updated during the testnet. Every change is listed at the end.

02The game

Your island

You place buildings on your island: lumberyards, quarries, fields, mills, mines, markets, houses and storehouses. Each one produces on its own. You collect what they make, spend it on upgrades, and raise the core of the island to unlock more.

The island keeps working while you are away. Buildings store what they produce until you return, so a few visits a day are enough. There is no need to keep the game open.

Four eras

Each era changes how the whole island looks and what its buildings are. Early levels take minutes; later ones take hours.

  1. I

    Village

    Core 1 to 3

    The Spark Altar and the first houses, fields and workshops.

  2. II

    Town

    Core 4 to 6

    A walled town with barns, mills and workshops.

  3. III

    Metropolis

    Core 7 to 9

    Towers, factories and a city that lights up at night.

  4. IV

    Star Age

    Core 10

    A star base, and the start of the endless game.

After the last era the game does not end: buildings and the core can be mastered without limit.

More than building

A farm island
Animals on a second island, joined to yours by a bridge.
Quests
New ones every day, week and month, and a chain of goals for each era.
Relics
Found on the island and equipped, each with a small bonus.
Ranks and a leaderboard
Rank comes from developing your island.
Pets, decorations, costumes
They change how the island looks, not how it performs.
Other islands
Visit other players, read your mailbox, redeem gift codes.

Spark Shards

Spark Shards are the in-game helper currency. They speed up construction and pay for a few extras such as relic scans and pets. They come from quests, chests, ranks and rewarded ads. They cannot be bought.

03Genesis Testnet Point

GTP is a point recorded on the game’s servers during the testnet. It shows how much a player has taken part.

  • No monetary value
  • Cannot be bought or sold
  • Cannot be transferred

How it is earned

GTP comes from one source only: a rewarded ad that the player chooses to watch, in the Telegram version of the game. Nothing else in the game gives GTP.

GTP per ad = base amount × island development up to 1.5 × GTP level up to 1.3

Levels

There are ten GTP levels, reached by total GTP collected. A higher level raises the per-ad multiplier slightly and allows more ads per day.

What GTP does not do

GTP does not make an island produce faster and it does not buy rank. Watching ads unlocks things that change how an island looks, such as temporary VIP badges and costumes, and nothing that changes how it performs.

The numbers in this section are the current testnet settings. They may change as the game is balanced.

04How the project is funded

Genesis Idle is free to play and has no in-game purchases. The project is paid for by attention, not by players’ wallets.

Ads never start on their own and never interrupt play. Sponsored placements inside the game, clearly marked as such, may be added later.

What this means for players

  • Nothing is sold to players, inside the game or outside it.
  • No amount of money buys production, rank or GTP.
  • Every ad is optional.

05Fair play

A reward is given only when three things agree.

The server decides
The same rule set runs in the game and on the server, and the server’s result is the one that counts. The game on a player’s device cannot grant itself resources, time or points.
Limits apply to everyone
Daily allowances, the six-hour window and the wait between ads are enforced by the server. Unusually fast or regular patterns are recorded for review.
Nothing to buy
There is no purchase that changes production or rank, so there is no paid advantage.
One measure for rank
The leaderboard is ordered by how developed an island is.

06Roadmap

The roadmap is given in phases without dates. Each phase begins when the one before it is ready.

  1. Done

    Foundations

    The game with its four eras, 50 languages, this website and the official channels.

  2. Next

    Telegram launch

    The game opens through the official bot. The testnet begins and GTP starts to be recorded.

  3. Then

    Testnet period

    The game is balanced with real players. Leaderboards and events run. Token details are published.

  4. Then

    Web version

    The same island, playable in a browser on computers and phones.

  5. Later

    After the testnet

    What was announced during the testnet is carried out, and the game keeps growing with new content.
